unsound, rebuilt around reading the call, not running it.

A designer, a UX lead and a critic each went through unsound and glint on their own. This page shows three directions you can compare screen by screen, four logo candidates, and the UX changes all three agreed on.

The panel

Where they agreed, and where they didn't

D

Designer

Take glint's system: named tokens, surface separation instead of borders, one "stage" moment, Radix primitives. Don't take its per-theme display fonts or its light-first default.

  • Proposed Booth, Margin and Tide
  • Recommended Tide (a glint sibling) plus Margin's highlighter
U

UX lead

The IA stopped at v0.7. Meetings features got bolted onto a dictation screen.

  • Six names for "to-dos" in five places
  • The live AI summary is never shown
  • Four "ask" boxes with two meanings
  • Proposed a sidebar, an Inbox and a vocabulary table
C

Critic deciding vote

Tide reads as a glint theme, not a brand. Light-first is hostile next to Zoom at 11pm. Serif ASR text makes raw speech look edited.

  • Cut Home; make Meetings the default view
  • Live view goes full-focus, with no sidebar
  • Proposed the "rotation" logo
  • Ship Booth
Recommendation Option 1, Booth: dark-first, a single amber accent, SF Pro, the rotation logo, Margin's highlighter for flagged moments, glint's token names and shadcn primitives under the hood. Every option below already includes the UX changes.
Three options

Same product, three personalities

Choose a screen and all three options switch together. Use "Alternate theme" to see each one in its second theme. Floating windows always stay dark because they sit over other apps.

Mocks are 1060-wide window crops
Logo

Four marks, tested at 16px and in the menu bar

The name means sound turned into something else. The two strongest marks say that literally: amber for sound, ink for text. The menu-bar strip uses each mark as a one-color template image, the way macOS draws it.

Type

Fonts, by job

All three options follow the same rule: reading text never changes with the theme. Transcripts stay sans because ASR output is fragmented speech, and a serif dresses it up as edited prose. A serif appears only on the AI summary, which really is prose (Options 2 and 3). Numbers always use tabular figures.

UX changes (in all three options)

Ten problems, ranked

  1. Six names for to-dos, in five places.Action items, follow-ups, reminders and requests all exist, and the tab called "tasks" doesn't hold your tasks. Fix: one Inbox with Ready to send, Due and Action items.
  2. The live view hides the AI summary.It shows only "notes ready through 12:40", and every transcript line repeats its speaker label. Fix: turn-grouped transcript, Summary rail, Jump to live.
  3. Meeting detail is an unranked 8-section scroll.The transcript comes last, speaker editing interrupts reading, and Delete has no confirmation. Fix: Summary, Transcript and Your notes tabs, a rail for actions, delete behind ··· with a confirmation.
  4. Four "ask" boxes with two meanings.Two of them run commands and two answer questions. Fix: "Ask unsound to…" for commands and ⌘K "Search or ask your meetings" for questions, never both on one screen.
  5. The Meetings page is a stack of banners.Copy like "3 audio ranges remaining · mic saved through 12.3s". Fix: status chips appear only when something is wrong, and details sit behind a disclosure.
  6. Settings are duplicated.The voice model is stored in two places, and the meeting language is borrowed from the Dictate tab. Fix: 5 groups instead of 11, one store each.
  7. The app opens on a dictation pill.Dictation actually happens through the hotkey in other apps. Fix: Meetings is the default view, and Dictations becomes a searchable library.
  8. Onboarding sets up half the product.It never mentions meetings, the system-audio permission or Calendar. Fix: 4 steps that cover both products.
  9. Every button looks the same.They are all 12.5px dim text, with mixed lowercase and Title Case, and --line is never defined. Fix: primary, secondary and quiet styles, with sentence case.
  10. The floating indicator only offers Stop."Call ended?" hides its explanation in a tooltip. Fix: add Flag, click to open the live view, and a real "Stop recording? Keep / Stop" prompt.

Plan

How to ship it without a big bang

  1. Tokens and primitives. Add Tailwind and glint's token names (rail/paper/ink/muted/line/accent/tint), plus faint, rec and hl. Port glint's Radix controls. Define the button tiers. Replace the hard-coded hex values in App.css step by step.
  2. Vocabulary and Inbox. Rename everything to the vocabulary above. Merge Tasks, FollowUps and ReminderInbox into Inbox. This is the biggest clarity gain for the least code.
  3. Live view. Focus mode, a turn-grouped transcript, a Summary/Requests rail, one composer, Jump to live, chips only when something is wrong.
  4. Detail view. Tabs plus a rail, citations that highlight their source lines, and ··· with delete confirmations.
  5. Sidebar shell. Meetings as the default view, a Dictations library replacing the tab and the drawer, and a contextual record card replacing the banners.
  6. Settings, onboarding and floating windows. 5 settings groups, onboarding that covers both products, Flag and the call-ended prompt on the indicator, and the new icon and tray glyph.
