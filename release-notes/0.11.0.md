## Who said what

- unsound can now read who is on a Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call (Meet in Chrome, Arc, Edge or Brave) and use those names for speakers. It needs Accessibility access and the participants panel open, and it only reads the list.
- On a one-to-one call the other person is named for you. In a group, the last unnamed voice is filled in once everyone else is named. Names you set yourself are never changed.
- When unsound isn't sure, renaming a speaker offers the people on the call and your calendar invitees as one-click suggestions.
- Names of people on the call are given to the speech model, so they are spelled correctly more often.

## No more echo on speakers

- When you take a call on speakers, the other side's voice no longer shows up again as something you said. unsound removes it from your microphone before transcribing. On headphones your audio is left exactly as it was.
- Dictating during a meeting is cleaned the same way, so the call isn't typed into your document.
- Saved microphone audio for a meeting is now the cleaned version.

## Speech models

- New: Parakeet v2 (English) and Parakeet v3 (25 European languages) run on the GPU. In our tests on public meeting recordings they made noticeably fewer errors than Whisper Large v3 Turbo and ran several times faster. Whisper stays the default.
- Parakeet can't use your dictionary or participant names to guide spelling, and doesn't cover Hindi and other non-European languages. For those, unsound uses a Whisper model if you have one downloaded and tells you if you don't.
- The previous Parakeet model could return empty text and has been replaced. If you were using it, unsound switches you to Parakeet v3 and asks you to download it (740 MB).
- New installs now start with Whisper Large v3 Turbo instead of Whisper Small. Your current choice is not changed.
- Fewer invented lines during silence ("Thanks for watching", "[Music]") and repeated-word loops are cleaned up.

## Permissions

- Setup has a new step that explains each permission unsound uses and lets you grant it there. Microphone and Accessibility are needed for dictation; Screen & System Audio Recording and Calendars are optional.
- Settings → Permissions shows every permission with its status and a button to grant it or open System Settings.
- If a needed permission is off, unsound tells you once at launch. Warnings about missing system audio or calendar access link to the Permissions page.
