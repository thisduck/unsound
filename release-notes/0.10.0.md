## A new look

- unsound has a new, calmer design: a warm light theme that switches to dark with your Mac, the Inter typeface, and a serif for summaries.
- Meetings, Dictations and Inbox sit in the title bar. The app opens on Meetings.
- When a call is detected, the Meetings screen offers to record it.

## Meetings

- The live view takes the whole window. It shows the summary as it builds, groups the transcript by speaker, and has a Jump to live button when you scroll back.
- Flag a moment with ⌘M or the Flag button. Your notes and "Ask unsound to…" share one box.
- A meeting opens on its summary, with action items, requests and questions below it. The transcript and your notes have their own tabs.
- Deleting a meeting now asks first.
- Search your meetings as you type, or press Return to ask the question and get an answer with sources.

## Inbox

- Requests unsound drafted, reminders that are due, and action items from every meeting are now in one place.
- The Inbox badge counts what needs you. Due reminders also appear at the top of Meetings.

## Dictations

- Every dictation is listed, newest first, with the selected one open beside it to copy or clean up again.

## Settings and setup

- Settings are grouped into General, Dictation, Meetings, Assistant and Models.
- Meeting models are set in one place, Settings → Meetings. You can also give meetings their own spoken language instead of following dictation.
- Setup covers both dictation and meetings, and destructive actions ask before deleting.

## Floating windows

- The recording indicator has a Flag button. Clicking it opens the live meeting.
- When a call ends, the indicator asks whether to stop recording, with Keep and Stop buttons.
- The call prompt now offers to record the call and get notes when it ends.
