## Back-to-back meetings

- Start a new meeting while the previous transcript finishes and its notes are generated.
- Previous meetings keep generating notes when you open another meeting or start recording again.
- Notes and processing results stay attached to their meeting without interrupting the next recording.

## Meeting controls

- A floating recording indicator shows elapsed time and a Stop button above other apps.
- Automatic recording prompts appear only for recognized meeting apps and supported meeting URLs.
